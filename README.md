## Highlighted Projects

### Quantitative Finance
- 📉 **Stochastic Volatility Comparison** (Python, C++): Compared GARCH, Heston, and path dependent volatility models for derivatives pricing and risk forecasting. GARCH proved the most stable and predictive across market regimes.
- 📈 **Stochastic Portfolio Optimizer** (Python): Regime switching allocator using Gaussian Markov Switching and CRRA utility across US, EU, and Chinese equities and Gold. Reached an 11.73x wealth index versus 5.56x for S&P 500 buy and hold.
- 🧮 **Asian Option Pricing** (Python): Priced Asian options under Black Scholes using closed form geometric and Monte Carlo arithmetic averaging. A geometric control variate cut estimator variance by 1,358x.
- 📊 **Bayesian Semiparametric Modeling of Index Returns** (Bocconi thesis, supervised by Prof. Antonio Lijoi): Modeled the conditional distribution of daily stock index returns with MCMC estimation, producing posterior predictive VaR and ES.
- 🌡️ **[Weather Derivatives Pricing](https://github.com/GianiRanzetti/Minerva---Weather-Derivatives)**: SQL scraping of historical weather data, LSTM temperature forecasting across U.S. metros, and backtesting of American calls on temperature indices.
- 📉 **[Momentum-Based Portfolio Optimization](https://github.com/Lorenzo-Giambra/BSDSA-Stock-Prediction)**: Dynamic SGD optimizer tuned via Bayesian search, with a trading dashboard for portfolio trends and momentum patterns.
- 🏦 **Bankruptcy Prediction Platform**: Led an ML pipeline (XGBoost, Random Forests) built on financial ratios and macroeconomic indicators.
